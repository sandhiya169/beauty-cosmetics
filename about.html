Our story

Nine years, one kitchen counter to two studios.

2016

A kitchen-counter first batch

Founder Ananya Rao hand-poured the first 40 jars of a centella cream in her Chennai kitchen, after years of reacting to everything else on the market.

2018

National launch, one hero serum

Stackly shipped nationally with a single bakuchiol serum — no expansion for expansion's sake, just one formula proven at clinical strength.

2020

Certified cruelty-free, first studio opens

We earned cruelty-free certification and opened our Besant Nagar studio, so people could touch and test before they bought.

2022

100,000 rituals shipped

The range grew to makeup and hair, built on the same rule: six actives, no filler, full ingredient decks published for every product.

2026

Two studios, 40+ countries

A second studio opened in Bengaluru, and Stackly now ships worldwide — still formulated and packed by the same small team.

What we hold to

Four rules we haven't broken.

Botanical integrity

Six plant actives, maximum, in every formula — nothing added just to fill a label.

Radical transparency

Full ingredient decks and sourcing notes published on every product page.

Small-batch craft

Nothing poured in batches over 500 units — it's slower, but it's how we catch problems early.

Cruelty-free, always

Certified since 2020. Never tested on animals, not once, not anywhere in the supply.
